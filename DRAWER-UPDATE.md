# Drawer update

- The right-hand volume/contents sidebar is hidden by default on every viewport width.
- Use the hamburger button in the header to open and close the drawer.
- Click outside the panel or press Escape to close it.
- The study content now occupies a centered full-width layout when the drawer is closed.
- The drawer is ARIA-labeled and toggles aria-expanded / aria-hidden.
- The website retains its existing font loader; local Montserrat Arabic fonts must be installed separately using the supplied setup helper and your licensed font archive.
